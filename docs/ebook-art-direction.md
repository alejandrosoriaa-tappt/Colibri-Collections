# Art Direction Brief — *Turn WhatsApp Business Into Your Best Sales Employee*

**Product:** Premium whitepaper-style ebook · PDF · $29 USD · reads on phone, tablet, PC
**Author/brand:** Alejandro Soria — NKUVO Labs
**Feel:** trustworthy, editorial, modern, uncluttered. Think *McKinsey whitepaper meets a friendly small-business coach.* Lots of white space, one strong accent, real device screenshots — never cheap clip-art.

---

## 1. Color palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Text / ink | Deep Pine | `#0B2721` | Body copy, dark UI frames |
| Primary dark | WhatsApp Teal | `#075E54` | Headlines, chapter titles, table headers |
| Secondary | Muted Teal | `#128C7E` | Eyebrows/kickers, subheads, captions |
| Action accent | Signal Green | `#25D366` | Rules, "phone" boxes, buttons, key highlights |
| Premium accent | Warm Gold | `#E0A83C` | "Did you know?" boxes, star/VIP, premium bits |
| Cool note | Slate Blue | `#2A6F97` | "On your computer" boxes only |
| Page | Warm Paper | `#FAF7F0` | Page background (whitepaper warmth) |
| Panel neutral | Sand | `#ECE5D8` | Example cards, dividers, table zebra |

**Rule of thumb:** teal + green do 80% of the work; gold is the "premium spice" (use sparingly); slate is *only* for desktop/computer notes so the reader learns the color code.

## 2. Typography

- **Headlines / chapter titles:** a warm high-contrast serif — **Fraunces** (or Source Serif 4 / Tiempos). Signals "editorial, considered."
- **Body:** a humanist sans — **Inter** (or Söhne / Neue Haas). Clean, trustworthy, great on screens.
- **Eyebrow/kicker** (the little "CHAPTER 4 ·" line): Inter, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.14em, Muted Teal.
- **Callout box titles:** Inter medium.
- Under every chapter title: a **44px × 3px gold rule** (the signature detail).

## 3. Layout system

- **Grid:** single column, generous margins (whitepaper). ~60–68 characters per line.
- **Editorial float (the look you liked):** image floats to one side at ~40–45% width; body text wraps beside it, then **reopens to full width** in the next paragraph. Alternate float side (right, then left) chapter to chapter for rhythm.
- **Callout boxes** (color-coded, left accent bar 4px, radius only on the open side):
  - 🟢 **Do It On Your Phone** — Signal Green bar, pale green fill `#E7F7EE`
  - 🔵 **On Your Computer** — Slate bar, pale blue fill `#E8F0F5`
  - 🟡 **Did you know? / Example / Reality Check** — Gold bar, pale gold fill `#FBF1DC`
  - ⬜ **Pro Tips / Action Plan** — Sand card with green check bullets
- **Playbook cards (Ch 11):** teal header band + industry icon, journey shown as a horizontal chip flow.
- **Infographics:** flat, 2-color max, lots of air (match diagram guidance).

## 5. Global style suffix (append to EVERY AI image prompt)

**STYLE = hand-drawn pencil / graphite whitepaper** (the chosen look):

> `hand-drawn pencil sketch illustration, fine graphite line work with soft shading and visible pencil texture, editorial whitepaper aesthetic, drawn on warm off-white #FAF7F0 paper, mostly graphite/grayscale with a single accent color — deep teal #075E54 and signal green #25D366 — used sparingly for one or two key elements, elegant, minimal, generous negative space, premium hand-crafted "consultant sketchbook" feel, no text, no words, no logos, no watermark`

Notes:
- Base everything in **graphite** (pencil gray). Bring in **teal/green as a spot accent** only (one element per illustration). Gold used even more rarely, for premium moments.
- Keep line work **light and confident**, not heavy or cartoonish. Think refined editorial sketch, not doodle.
- Consistency comes from the pencil texture + single-accent rule across every image.

Keep every prompt in this DNA so the whole book feels like one system.

**✅ STYLE LOCKED (approved):** the pencil style is final. Reference master = the approved cover (smiling phone at a reception desk, generic chat bubble — NO commercial logos/trademarks — single muted teal-green `#128C7E` accent applied as colored pencil, graphite base, warm `#FAF7F0` paper). The approved follow-up "paper planes" interior is the reference for how subtle the accent should be. Every image: graphite base + ONE colored-pencil teal-green accent, no brand logos of any kind.

## 7. Production notes
- Export PDF at screen-friendly proportions (not letter/A4) so it reads well on phones — e.g. a 16:10-ish page or a reflowable layout.
- Every image needs a light and a dark-friendly version? No — PDF is fixed; design on Warm Paper only.
- Keep file size reasonable (compress images) so it downloads fast after purchase.
- Consistency check before export: gold used sparingly, slate ONLY on computer notes, every chapter title has its gold rule.

Chapter 1 — Get the App (and Why It's Not Your Personal WhatsApp)

Download, verify, and understand the difference

WhatsApp Business is a separate, free app. This is the part almost nobody explains. It is not the same app you use to text your family. It's a different app — with a different icon — that you download for free from the App Store or Google Play. It gives you business tools (greeting messages, catalog, labels, quick replies) that regular WhatsApp simply doesn't have. You can use it with the same phone number you already have, and your customers won't notice any difference on their side — they just message you as usual.

📱 Do It On Your Phone — Get WhatsApp Business (Free)
1. Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
2. Search for "WhatsApp Business" and tap Get / Install. Look for the icon with the letter B inside the speech bubble — that's the business version.
3. Open it and verify your phone number. If you already use regular WhatsApp on this number, the app will offer to move your chats over — tap yes.
4. Fill in your business name and category. Done.

Note: you can keep your personal WhatsApp on a second number if you like, but most solo owners simply turn their existing number into their business number.

💻 On your computer, too. WhatsApp Business also has a desktop app (Windows/Mac) and a browser version at web.whatsapp.com. You'll set up most tools on your phone, but once they're ready you can reply from a full keyboard.

That's it — you now have the tool. The rest of Part 1 is about switching on each feature, one at a time.

Chapter 2 — Set Up a Profile Customers Trust

Your profile is your digital storefront

Before a customer reads your first reply, they look at your profile. A complete, professional profile answers the silent question "Can I trust this business?" — so fill in every field.

📱 Do It On Your Phone — Find Your Business Tools
Everything in this book lives in one place. Learn to get there once:
- Android: open WhatsApp Business → tap the ⋮ (three dots, top right) → Business tools.
- iPhone: open WhatsApp Business → tap Settings (bottom right) → Business tools.

To edit your name, photo, description, address, and website, tap your business name at the top of Settings → Business profile.

Fill in each field the way a customer would want to see it:

  • Name: your business name, clear and searchable (e.g. Bright Smile Dental), not a nickname.
  • Photo: a clean logo, or a professional headshot if you're a solo provider. People buy from people.
  • Description: what you do, who you help, and why choose you — in one or two lines.
  • Address, website, email, hours: complete them all. Missing information makes customers suspicious.

Set honest business hours so customers know when to expect a reply — and so your Away Message (Chapter 3) can switch on automatically.

📱 Do It On Your Phone — Set Your Business Hours
1. Go to your Business profile → tap Business hours (Android) / Hours (iPhone).
2. Choose Open with hours.
3. Tap each day to set opening and closing times. Turn off the days you're closed.
4. Tap Save.

Chapter 3 — Turn On Your Auto-Replies

Greet every customer, even while you sleep

Two settings make your business feel responsive around the clock: a greeting message for new chats and an away message for after hours. Set them once and they work forever.

The greeting message is your digital handshake. It welcomes every new customer before you ever touch your phone.

📱 Do It On Your Phone — Turn On Your Greeting Message
1. Open Business tools → Greeting message.
2. Turn on Send greeting message.
3. Tap the text to edit it. Paste your welcome message.
4. Tap Recipients (default: Everyone who messages you for the first time).
5. Tap Save.

The away message keeps customers informed when you're closed — no guilt about not answering at midnight.

📱 Do It On Your Phone — Turn On Your Away Message
1. Open Business tools → Away message.
2. Turn on Send away message.
3. Paste your away message.
4. Tap Schedule → Outside of business hours (it uses the hours you set in Chapter 2 — the easiest option).
5. Tap Recipients, then Save.

Keep both messages warm and human. Avoid robotic lines like "Your inquiry has been received." Sound like a real person who's glad to help.

Chapter 4 — Save Your Answers with Quick Replies

Stop typing the same thing every day

You answer the same questions constantly: your hours, your prices, your address, whether you take insurance. Save each answer once as a Quick Reply, then drop it into any chat by typing /.

📱 Do It On Your Phone — Create a Quick Reply
1. Open Business tools → Quick replies.
2. Tap Add (Android) / the + (iPhone).
3. In Message, type the full response (e.g. your hours or pricing).
4. In Shortcut, type a keyword like hours or pricing (no spaces).
5. Tap Save.

To use it: in any chat, type / followed by the shortcut (e.g. /hours), then pick it from the list. 💻 On your computer it works the same way with the / key.

Start with five: hours, pricing, address, booking, and insurance (or whatever your customers ask most). Save two minutes per chat, thirty chats a day — that's an hour back, every day.

Chapter 5 — Build Your Catalog

A one-tap showroom for your services

Instead of describing your services over and over, build a catalog once and send it with a single tap. Think of it as a digital showroom that lives inside the chat.

📱 Do It On Your Phone — Create Your Catalog
1. Open Business tools → Catalog.
2. Tap Add item (Android) / Add new item (iPhone).
3. Add one or more photos of the service or product.
4. Fill in Name, Price (or leave blank and add "Starting at…" in the description), and Description.
5. Optional: add a Link (your website/booking page).
6. Tap Save. Repeat for each service.

To send it: inside any chat, tap the paperclip / + → Catalog → choose items → Send.

Give each item a specific name and a clean photo. "New Patient Special — Exam, Cleaning & X-Rays" sells far better than "Cleaning."

Chapter 6 — Get Your Chat Link & QR Code

Make it effortless to start a conversation

Every extra step loses customers. WhatsApp Business gives you a short link and a QR code so anyone can start a chat in one tap — put them everywhere.

📱 Do It On Your Phone — Get Your Link & QR Code
1. Open Business tools → Short link.
2. Copy your ready-to-share link (e.g. wa.me/1210XXXXXXX) for your website, ads, and social bios.
3. Tap Edit default message to pre-fill what customers see, e.g. "Hi! I'd like more information about…".
4. Tap QR code → Share or Save to download it for print.

Put the QR code on business cards, invoices, your storefront window, your vehicle, and email signatures. Every scan starts a conversation.

Chapter 7 — Organize Every Customer with Labels

A simple CRM you already own

As chats pile up, they all start to look the same. Labels turn your inbox into an organized pipeline — you always know who's new, who's waiting, and who owes you money.

📱💻 Do It On Your Phone — Create and Assign Labels
- Create: ⋮ / Settings → Business tools → Labels → Add new label → name it, pick a color → Save.
- Assign: press and hold a chat → tap the label icon → choose the label(s).
- View one stage: tap the Labels filter at the top of your chats and pick a label to see only those customers. Works on the desktop app too.

Start with a simple pipeline and give every conversation exactly one label:

Each morning, open Follow-up needed and Payment pending first. That's where tomorrow's revenue is hiding.

Chapter 8 — Get Paid with Payment Links

Collect without the awkward conversation

Most late payments aren't intentional — customers simply forget. Make paying effortless by dropping a payment link right into the chat, and save it as a Quick Reply so it's one tap away.

📱 Do It On Your Phone — Keep Your Payment Link Handy
1. Set up a payment link with your provider (Stripe, Square, PayPal, etc.).
2. Save it as a Quick Reply: Business tools → Quick replies → Add → paste your link → shortcut pay → Save.
3. In any chat, type /pay and send. The link drops right in — no searching.

Use the same simple flow with every customer so cash flow becomes predictable:

Always confirm when a payment arrives ("Thank you! We've received your payment.") — it takes five seconds and builds trust.

Chapter 9 — A Day with WhatsApp Business

What it looks like in real businesses

Here's how the pieces you just set up work together in everyday situations. Adapt the messages to your own business.

A dental office. A new patient messages after hours. Your away message replies instantly with your hours and emergency number. In the morning you send /insurance and your catalog item for the new-patient special. You label them New Lead, then Appointment Confirmed once they book. After the visit, you send /pay, then a thank-you and a review request.

A roofing contractor. A homeowner scans the QR code on your truck and messages "Can you look at a leak?" Your greeting welcomes them and asks for their address and a photo. You send an estimate, label them Quote Sent, and follow up in two days. When they approve, you collect a deposit with /pay.

A salon or med spa. A client asks "How much is a facial?" You reply with /pricing and your catalog. You book them, send a reminder the day before, and after the visit invite them to your loyalty offer — all labeled and organized so no one slips through the cracks.

Different businesses, same toolkit: greet, answer with quick replies, show the catalog, organize with labels, and get paid with a link.

Chapter 10 — Let AI Write Your Messages

Never stare at a blank screen again

When you're not sure what to write, let AI draft it. Open ChatGPT, describe what you need, and paste the result into WhatsApp — then adjust it to sound like you and save it as a Quick Reply.

Prompts you'll use again and again:

  • Write a warm WhatsApp greeting message for a [your business] in [your city]. Keep it friendly and professional.
  • Rewrite this reply to sound warmer and clearer: [paste your text].
  • Write a friendly, pressure-free follow-up for a customer who got a quote three days ago.
  • Write a polite payment reminder for an overdue invoice.
  • Give me five quick replies for a [your business].
📱💻 Tip — Close the loop. When AI writes a response you love, paste it straight into Business tools → Quick replies so it's ready with a / shortcut forever. On your computer, you can even copy a whole chat into ChatGPT and ask it to summarize the customer's needs.

AI generates; you decide. Always give it a quick read before you send — your personality is your advantage.

Chapter 11 — Follow Up Like a Pro

Staying in touch, without being pushy

Most sales happen after more than one message, yet most businesses stop after the first. You don't need a complicated system — just a simple rhythm and the tools you already set up.

After you send a quote, label the customer Follow-up needed and check in on a gentle schedule:

Keep each message helpful, not needy. Save them as Quick Replies (/checkin, /tip, /lastcall) so a follow-up takes one tap. Every morning, open your Follow-up needed label and message everyone in it. That five-minute habit is where forgotten sales come back to life.

Closing — Your First Week

You don't have to do everything at once. Set up one thing a day, and by the end of the week WhatsApp Business is doing real work for you.

A simple first week:

  • ☐ Day 1 — Install WhatsApp Business and complete your profile (Chapters 1–2).
  • ☐ Day 2 — Turn on your greeting and away messages (Chapter 3).
  • ☐ Day 3 — Create five Quick Replies (Chapter 4).
  • ☐ Day 4 — Build your catalog and get your QR code (Chapters 5–6).
  • ☐ Day 5 — Create your labels and label every active chat (Chapter 7).
  • ☐ Day 6 — Set up your payment link (Chapter 8).
  • ☐ Day 7 — Add AI to your workflow and start your daily follow-up habit (Chapters 10–11).

That's it. No new software, no extra staff, no code — just the free app already in your pocket, finally working the way it should.
